Reading the tables. AUC = area under the ROC curve, mean over the 50 test folds (chance = .50). The other
numbers come from each repeat's pooled out-of-fold predictions, averaged over repeats: PR-AUC = average
precision for "loss" (chance = the share with loss); recall per class and balanced accuracy at 0.5; BSS =
Brier skill score against predicting the training prevalence (SMOTENC rebalances the training data, so its
probabilities are not calibrated: AUC measures ranking, BSS penalises the shift). For the count: Q2 = 1 -
MSE / Var(y) per test fold (0 = no better than predicting the mean), MAE and RMSE. No interval is given for a
single model: with 7-8 people per test fold a per-fold interval under-covers. Chance is judged by the
permutation tests; comparisons between models use paired, corrected intervals.
